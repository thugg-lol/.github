### ✦ What you can do

- 🎨 **Design every layer** — background, name, card, cursor and badges. Each one is yours to change, not a fixed template.
- 🎵 **Music on your page** — add tracks to your page's player so visitors hear your vibe.
- 🔗 **All your links in one place** — socials, games, streams and anything else you want people to find.
- 🟢 **Live status** — your Discord presence, what you're playing on Spotify or Steam, and a LIVE badge for Twitch and Kick.
- 📈 **Analytics** — see your views, clicks, referrers, countries and devices.
- 🗂️ **File hosting** — upload images, GIFs, video and audio through ShareX or the desktop app, with short links that can expire.
- 🏷️ **Username market** — buy, sell or auction handles. A username is an asset you own.
- 🌐 **More addresses** — get your name on thugg.tech and thugger.fun too.

### ✦ Get started

1. Claim your handle at **[thugg.lol/register](https://thugg.lol/register)**
2. Design your page in the editor
3. Share one link — `thugg.lol/yourname`

### ✦ Plans

The page is **free forever**. **Premium** and **Creator** are one-time payments for lifetime access — nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel. Premium can also be unlocked through referrals.

Compare everything on the **[pricing page](https://thugg.lol/pricing)**.
